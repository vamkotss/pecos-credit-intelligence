# CREDIT MEMORANDUM
## PCP-0012 | Red River Fabrication

---

## FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

Red River Fabrication generated revenue of $33,550,507 in FY2025, representing minimal growth of 0.5% year-over-year [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1]. Gross margin remained stable at 51.4% [CALCULATED METRICS], but EBITDA declined 5.1% to $2,083,085, reflecting operating expense pressure [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] [CALCULATED METRICS]. EBITDA margin compressed to 6.2% [CALCULATED METRICS]. Net income fell to $653,132, a net margin of 1.9% [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] [CALCULATED METRICS].

Operating cash flow of $1,528,479 was consumed by capital expenditures of $1,549,968 and debt service, leaving minimal free cash flow [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]. Cash and equivalents declined 69.1% over the period to $574,939 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [CALCULATED METRICS].

## BALANCE SHEET

Total assets of $16,275,505 are supported by equity of $8,906,721, representing a debt-to-assets ratio of 45.3% [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2]. Current assets of $7,417,809 exceed current liabilities of $3,590,327, yielding a current ratio of 2.07x [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [CALCULATED METRICS].

Accounts receivable of $3,125,252 represent 9.3% of annual revenue [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2]. Receivables over 90 days total $163,280, or 5.2% of the portfolio [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1] [CALCULATED METRICS].

## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

Total interest-bearing debt is $4,314,086, comprising a current portion of $535,629 and long-term debt of $3,778,457 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [CALCULATED METRICS]. The proposed $4,200,000 facility would refinance the maturing term loan and fund equipment replacement [01_loan_application.pdf#p1].

**Leverage:** Total debt-to-EBITDA is 2.07x [CALCULATED METRICS]. This metric is acceptable and below the 3.5x policy threshold. No structural mitigant is required on leverage grounds.

**Coverage:** Debt Service Coverage Ratio is 0.40x, calculated as (EBITDA $2,083,085 − capex $1,549,968 − taxes $173,617) / (interest $366,995 + principal $535,629) [CALCULATED METRICS]. This ratio **fails the 1.25x minimum requirement** by a material margin. The company cannot service its current debt load from operating cash flow after funding necessary capital expenditures.

Interest coverage of 5.68x is adequate [CALCULATED METRICS], but this metric masks the structural cash flow deficit evident in the DSCR calculation.

---

## FINDINGS

1. **Deteriorating profitability:** EBITDA declined 5.1% despite flat revenue growth, signaling operational stress. Net margin of 1.9% provides minimal cushion [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] [CALCULATED METRICS].

2. **Severe cash flow deficit:** Operating cash flow of $1,528,479 is insufficient to cover capex of $1,549,968 and debt service of $902,624 (interest plus principal). The company is drawing cash reserves to fund operations [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3].

3. **Liquidity deterioration:** Cash declined 69.1% to $574,939, a level inadequate to absorb operational disruption or fund the stated equipment replacement program [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [CALCULATED METRICS].

4. **Extreme customer concentration:** Five customers represent 100.0% of receivables, with the largest customer (Brazos Partners) at 32.4% [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1] [CALCULATED METRICS]. Loss of any major customer would create immediate liquidity stress.

5. **Aging receivables:** 5.2% of receivables are over 90 days old, indicating collection risk in a concentrated customer base [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1] [CALCULATED METRICS].

6. **Capex dependency:** The company requires $1.55M annually in capital expenditures to maintain operations, a level that cannot be funded from free cash flow [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3].

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DECLINE**

The borrower fails the coverage requirement. A DSCR of 0.40x indicates the company cannot service debt from operating cash flow after funding necessary capital expenditures. Refinancing existing debt at $4.2M would perpetuate a structurally unsustainable capital structure.

The combination of declining EBITDA, severe cash flow deficit, deteriorating liquidity, and extreme customer concentration creates unacceptable credit risk for a $4.2M senior secured facility. The borrower requires operational improvement and customer diversification before this facility can be supported.
