## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

Total interest-bearing debt is $8,508,027 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2]. Pro forma leverage including the requested $12,800,000 facility would be approximately 4.0x total debt to EBITDA. Current leverage stands at 1.58x [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2].

**Leverage Policy Requirement:** Post-funding leverage of approximately 4.0x exceeds the 3.5x threshold and requires a structural mitigant. No mitigant is identified in the submission.

Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) is 2.76x, calculated as (EBITDA – capex – taxes) / (interest + principal) [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]. This exceeds the 1.25x minimum requirement.

Interest coverage is 7.72x (EBITDA / interest expense) [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1], well above acceptable thresholds.

## FINDINGS

**Strengths:**
- Consistent revenue growth (11.2% YoY) and strong EBITDA growth (24.1% YoY) demonstrate operational momentum [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1].
- Healthy gross margin of 45.7% and EBITDA margin of 10.4% [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1].
- Strong current ratio of 4.20x and adequate DSCR of 2.76x [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2].
- Established business (operating since 1989) with stable employment base of 274 [01_loan_application.pdf#p1].
- Positive equity position of $17,176,913 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2].

**Weaknesses:**
- **Customer concentration risk:** Top five customers represent 90.9% of accounts receivable, with the largest customer (Red River Distribution) at 34.2% [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1]. Loss of a major customer would materially impair cash flow.
- **Receivables quality:** 5.1% of receivables are over 90 days past due [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1].
- **Pro forma leverage:** The requested $12,800,000 facility would result in total debt of approximately $21.3 million, or 4.0x EBITDA, exceeding the 3.5x policy threshold without identified structural mitigation [01_loan_application.pdf#p1].
- **Unaudited financials:** Statements are reviewed but not audited, limiting assurance on reported figures [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].
- **Related party lease:** Operating facility is leased from an entity controlled by Anselmo Ibarra; management asserts the rate is at market, but no independent verification is provided [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DEFER**

The borrower demonstrates solid operational performance and adequate current leverage metrics. However, the requested facility would push total debt to approximately 4.0x EBITDA, exceeding the 3.5x policy threshold. No structural mitigant (asset sale, equity injection, or debt reduction) has been proposed to address this overage.

Before proceeding, require: (1) identification and documentation of a structural mitigant to bring pro forma leverage to 3.5x or below, or (2) reduction of the facility request to approximately $9.5 million to maintain 3.5x leverage; (3) customer concentration mitigation plan addressing the 90.9% top-five concentration; and (4) independent verification of the related-party lease rate.

A site visit and detailed discussion of use of proceeds (refinance of seller notes and distribution centre lease) should also be conducted to confirm the strategic rationale and cash flow impact.
