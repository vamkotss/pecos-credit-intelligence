EBITDA of $6,731,180 grew 18.9% year-over-year [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] [Calculated Metrics]. Net income was $3,149,964, yielding a net margin of 8.2% [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] [Calculated Metrics].

Operating cash flow for FY2025 was $4,295,885 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]. Cash and equivalents increased 71.8% over the period to $4,422,752 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [Calculated Metrics]. The borrower reports no material adverse change since fiscal year end [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].

Financial statements are prepared internally and reviewed annually by an independent accounting firm but are not audited [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].

## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

Total interest-bearing debt is $15,345,452 [Calculated Metrics]. Pro forma total debt including the requested $22,100,000 facility would be $37,445,452.

**Current leverage:** Total debt / EBITDA is 2.28x [Calculated Metrics], well below the 3.5x policy threshold.

**Pro forma leverage:** Post-funding, total debt / EBITDA would be approximately 5.56x ($37,445,452 / $6,731,180), materially exceeding the 3.5x policy threshold. No structural mitigant is identified in the submission.

**Debt service coverage:** Current DSCR is 1.81x [Calculated Metrics], exceeding the 1.25x minimum requirement. However, this calculation uses existing debt service. Pro forma DSCR incorporating the new facility's debt service cannot be computed from the provided figures, as the proposed term, rate, and amortization schedule are not specified.

Interest coverage (EBITDA / interest expense) is 4.87x [Calculated Metrics].

## FINDINGS

**Strengths:**
- Stable, long-established business (56 years in operation) with consistent revenue and improving EBITDA trajectory.
- Strong gross margin of 49.1% and EBITDA margin of 17.5% [Calculated Metrics].
- Excellent current ratio of 3.19x and positive operating cash flow of $4.3M [Calculated Metrics] [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3].
- Debt reduction trend: long-term debt declined from $16.5M (FY2023) to $14.2M (FY2025) [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2].

**Weaknesses:**
- **Extreme customer concentration:** Top five customers represent 100.0% of accounts receivable ($4,318,617 of $4,318,619 total) [Calculated Metrics]. Largest customer (Caprock Fabrication) is 25.7% of AR [Calculated Metrics]. This concentration creates material revenue and cash flow risk.
- **Receivables quality:** 5.9% of AR is over 90 days old [Calculated Metrics], indicating some collection friction.
- **Leverage trajectory:** The requested $22.1M facility would drive pro forma leverage to approximately 5.56x, far exceeding policy and creating structural risk.
- **Unaudited financials:** Statements are reviewed but not audited, limiting assurance [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].
- **Related-party lease:** Operating facility is leased from an entity controlled by majority owner Priya Hollingsworth [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].
- **Draft financials exist:** Document 10_financial_statements_draft.pdf is marked DRAFT and shows different EBITDA ($7,186,895 vs. $6,731,180 in the authoritative version), confirming reliance on the authoritative FY2025 statements [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1].

**Use of proceeds:** The facility is requested to acquire a competitor's book of business and provide working capital headroom [01_loan_application.pdf#p1]. No detail on the acquisition target, purchase price, or integration plan is provided.
