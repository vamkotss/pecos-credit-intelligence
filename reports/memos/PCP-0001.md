## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

Total interest-bearing debt is $8,025,829, comprising $615,848 current and $7,409,981 long-term [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2]. Existing debt consists of a BancFirst equipment term loan ($3,079,242 balance, 6.85%, due 2026) and a Southwest Bank revolving line ($4,946,587 balance, 9.42%, due 2026) [04_debt_schedule.pdf#p1].

**Leverage: 3.12x.** Total debt to EBITDA is 3.12x [CALCULATED METRICS]. This is below the 3.5x policy threshold and requires no structural mitigant.

**Coverage: 1.45x.** Debt service coverage ratio (EBITDA less capex and taxes, divided by interest and principal) is 1.45x [CALCULATED METRICS]. This exceeds the 1.25x minimum requirement. Interest coverage is 3.69x [CALCULATED METRICS].

**Pro forma leverage post-facility.** The requested $6,200,000 facility would increase total debt to $14,225,829. Pro forma leverage would be 5.52x ($14,225,829 / $2,575,918), materially above policy. No structural mitigant is identified in the application.

## FINDINGS

1. **Revenue and profitability trajectory.** Revenue growth of 3.0% is modest; EBITDA contracted 14.6% despite gross margin stability, indicating operating expense pressure. Net margin of 3.6% is thin.

2. **Leverage deterioration.** Current leverage of 3.12x is acceptable. However, the requested $6.2M facility would push pro forma leverage to 5.52x, a material policy breach with no identified mitigation.

3. **Coverage adequacy.** Current DSCR of 1.45x meets the 1.25x floor. Pro forma DSCR cannot be computed without pro forma debt service assumptions, but the magnitude of leverage increase suggests coverage would deteriorate materially.

4. **Customer concentration.** Five customers represent 100% of receivables, with the largest at 33.3%. This concentration creates revenue and cash flow volatility risk, particularly given the oilfield equipment distribution sector's cyclicality.

5. **Existing debt maturity.** Both existing facilities mature in 2026, creating refinancing risk within the proposed 5-year loan term.

6. **Liquidity and working capital.** Current ratio of 4.11x and cash position of $1,972,700 are strong. Receivables ageing is acceptable (2.3% over 90 days).

7. **Financial statement quality.** Statements are internally prepared and reviewed annually by an independent accounting firm but are not audited [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]. A superseded version of FY2023 statements exists [03_financial_statements_superseded.pdf#p1]; figures used herein are from the authoritative comparative statements [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf].

8. **Use of proceeds.** The facility is requested to refinance seller notes and support a new distribution centre lease. No detail on seller note terms or lease obligations is provided.

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DECLINE**

The requested $6,200,000 facility would result in pro forma leverage of 5.52x, exceeding the 3.5x policy threshold by 2.02x with no structural mitigant identified. While current leverage and coverage metrics are acceptable, the magnitude of the facility relative to EBITDA is incompatible with Pecos Capital Partners' risk parameters for middle-market lending. The borrower's modest revenue growth, EBITDA contraction, thin net margins, and extreme customer concentration compound the leverage concern. 

The borrower should be advised that a smaller facility—approximately $3.5M to $4.0M—would maintain pro forma leverage near 3.5x and may be approvable subject to satisfactory due diligence on use of proceeds, seller note terms, and lease obligations.
