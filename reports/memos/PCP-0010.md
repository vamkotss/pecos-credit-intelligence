## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

Total interest-bearing debt is $18,842,972 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2]. Leverage is 2.54x total debt to EBITDA, below the 3.5x policy threshold and requiring no structural mitigant [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1].

Debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) is 1.33x, calculated as (EBITDA – capex – taxes) / (interest + principal) = ($7,430,731 – $1,764,432 – $919,774) / ($1,631,916 + $1,945,597) [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]. This falls below the 1.25x minimum coverage requirement by 8 basis points and fails the policy threshold.

Interest coverage is 4.55x (EBITDA / interest expense) [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1].

## FINDINGS

**Receivables Quality and Concentration Risk.** Accounts receivable of $5,351,980 show acceptable ageing: 60.3% current, 23.7% aged 31–90 days, and 5.0% over 90 days [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1]. However, customer concentration is elevated. The largest customer (Cimarron Enterprises) represents 26.5% of receivables, and the top five customers represent 80.9% of total receivables [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1]. This concentration creates material credit risk in the event of customer loss or deterioration.

**Debt Structure.** Existing debt comprises four facilities totaling $18,842,972: a $5,112,350 revolving line of credit (9.01%), equipment term loan of $4,049,481 (9.22%), owner-occupied CRE mortgage of $6,280,178 (7.13%), and Term Loan A of $3,400,963 (7.91%) [04_debt_schedule.pdf#p1]. The proposed $19,900,000 facility would refinance the maturing Term Loan A (due 2026) and fund equipment replacement [01_loan_application.pdf#p1]. Pro forma total debt would be approximately $34.7M, resulting in pro forma leverage of approximately 4.67x EBITDA—materially above policy.

**Coverage Shortfall.** The current DSCR of 1.33x marginally exceeds the 1.25x minimum but provides minimal cushion. Pro forma DSCR incorporating the new facility's debt service would deteriorate further, likely falling below policy.

**Related Party Transaction.** The operating facility is leased from an entity controlled by Delphine Hollingsworth at rates management believes to be market [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]. This related-party lease should be verified for arm's-length pricing and subordinated to the senior lien position.

**Financial Statement Qualification.** Statements are reviewed but not audited, limiting assurance on reported figures [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DECLINE**

The facility request cannot be approved under current policy. Pro forma leverage would exceed 3.5x without a structural mitigant, and current DSCR already fails the 1.25x coverage requirement. Customer concentration (80.9% in top five) creates material obligor risk. The borrower should be advised to:

1. Reduce the facility request to $12–14M to maintain pro forma leverage below 3.5x and DSCR above 1.25x
