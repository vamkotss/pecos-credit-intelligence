## FINDINGS

1. **Use of Proceeds:** The requested $11,900,000 is stated to fund a partner buyout and consolidate two existing facilities [01_loan_application.pdf#p1]. No detail is provided on the buyout mechanics, valuation, or consolidation plan. The submission does not clarify whether the consolidation will generate cost savings or operational synergies.

2. **Leverage Concern:** Pro forma leverage of 4.97x EBITDA materially exceeds the 3.5x policy threshold. No structural mitigant (asset sale, equity injection, EBITDA step-up, or cash sweep) is proposed or documented.

3. **Customer Concentration Risk:** Top five customers represent 84.0% of accounts receivable, with the largest customer (Llano Manufacturing) at 33.7% [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1]. Loss of a major customer would materially impair cash flow and debt service capacity.

4. **Receivables Ageing:** 6.4% of receivables are over 90 days past due, which is moderate but warrants monitoring [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1].

5. **Financial Statement Preparation:** Statements are prepared internally and reviewed annually by an independent accounting firm but are not audited [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]. This is acceptable for a middle-market borrower of this size but limits assurance.

6. **Related Party Transaction:** The operating facility is leased from an entity controlled by Marisol Garza at a rate management believes to be at market [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]. No independent appraisal of market rent is provided.

7. **Pro Forma Debt Service:** The submission does not include a pro forma debt schedule, amortization assumptions, or pro forma cash flow statement for the requested facility. Pro forma DSCR cannot be verified.
