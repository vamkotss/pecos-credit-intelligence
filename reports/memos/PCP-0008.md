## FINDINGS

1. **Use of proceeds:** The facility is requested to fund a partner buyout and consolidate two facilities [01_loan_application.pdf#p1]. No detail on the buyout mechanics, purchase price allocation, or post-consolidation synergies has been provided.

2. **Ownership and control:** Corinne Ibarra owns 100% of the borrower; Terrence Escamilla is listed as 0% owner but is described as active in the business [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]. The buyout structure and post-transaction ownership are not specified.

3. **Related-party lease:** The operating facility is leased from an entity controlled by Corinne Ibarra at a rate management believes to be at market [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]. No lease agreement or independent valuation of market rate has been provided.

4. **Financial statements:** Statements are prepared internally and reviewed but not audited [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].

5. **Customer concentration risk:** 91.0% of receivables are concentrated in five customers, with the largest representing 36.4% [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1]. Loss of Pecan Creek Supply Co. would materially impair debt service capacity.

6. **Leverage constraint:** Pro forma leverage of approximately 4.34x exceeds policy. The borrower's current EBITDA generation cannot support this debt level without structural mitigation.

7. **Competitive tension:** The broker email indicates two other lenders are reviewing the deal, creating time pressure [07_broker_email_thread.pdf#p1].

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DEFER**

The facility request cannot proceed under current terms without material modifications:

- **Leverage mitigation required:** Reduce facility size, require equity injection, or obtain commitment to debt paydown/asset sale sufficient to bring pro forma leverage to 3.5x or below.
- **Use of proceeds clarity:** Provide detailed buyout structure, post-transaction ownership, and consolidation plan with identified synergies.
- **Customer concentration:** Obtain personal guarantees from Corinne Ibarra and Terrence Escamilla; consider covenant limiting single-customer concentration.
- **Related-party lease:** Provide executed lease agreement and independent market-rate valuation.

DSCR and interest coverage are adequate. The business is stable and cash-generative. With leverage addressed and use of proceeds clarified, this credit is fundable at $3M–$40M facility size. Request revised application with the above items before proceeding to credit committee.
