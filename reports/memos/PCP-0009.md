# CREDIT MEMORANDUM
**PCP-0009: Nueces Distribution**

---

## FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

Nueces Distribution generated revenue of $14,312,877 in FY2025, representing growth of 5.9% over the prior year [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1]. Gross profit was $4,319,074, yielding a gross margin of 30.2% [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1]. 

EBITDA declined to $1,267,547, a contraction of 12.5% year-over-year [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1], reflecting an EBITDA margin of 8.9% [CALCULATED METRICS]. Operating expenses increased to $3,051,527 in FY2025 from $2,627,899 in FY2024, outpacing revenue growth. Net income fell to $426,230 from $562,318 in the prior year, a net margin of 3.0% [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1].

Operating cash flow was $688,724 in FY2025 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]. The borrower incurred capital expenditures of $604,194 and made debt repayments of $376,298, with distributions to members of $57,859 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3].

## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

Total interest-bearing debt is $4,472,918, comprising current portion of $376,298 and long-term debt of $4,096,620 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [CALCULATED METRICS]. Existing facilities include a revolving line of credit ($1,422,996), owner-occupied CRE mortgage ($1,277,356), Term Loan A ($933,675), and Equipment Term Loan ($838,891) [04_debt_schedule.pdf#p1].

**Leverage: Total debt / EBITDA is 3.53x** [CALCULATED METRICS]. This exceeds the 3.5x policy threshold and requires a structural mitigant. The requested $3,100,000 facility would increase total debt to approximately $7,572,918, resulting in pro forma leverage of 5.97x—materially above policy.

**Coverage: DSCR is 0.76x** [CALCULATED METRICS], calculated as (EBITDA $1,267,547 – capex $604,194 – taxes $113,301) / (interest $347,237 + principal $376,298). This falls substantially below the 1.25x minimum requirement. Interest coverage stands at 3.65x [CALCULATED METRICS], which is adequate in isolation but does not offset the debt service shortfall.

---

## FINDINGS

1. **Deteriorating profitability.** EBITDA declined 12.5% despite 5.9% revenue growth, signaling margin compression and rising operating costs. Net income fell 24.2% year-over-year.

2. **Weak debt service capacity.** DSCR of 0.76x indicates the borrower cannot service existing debt from operating cash flow after capital expenditure and tax obligations. The facility request would worsen this position materially.

3. **Leverage above policy threshold.** Current leverage of 3.53x exceeds the 3.5x policy limit. Pro forma leverage post-facility would reach approximately 5.97x, unsustainable for a middle-market lender.

4. **Cash depletion.** Cash declined 64.4% over the period to $238,338, reflecting negative free cash flow. Operating cash generation of $688,724 is insufficient to cover capex ($604,194), debt service ($376,298 principal + $347,237 interest), and distributions ($57,859).

5. **Customer concentration risk.** The largest customer (Cimarron Fabrication) represents 36.5% of accounts receivable; the top five customers represent 90.1% [05_ar_aging_and_concentration.pdf#p1] [CALCULATED METRICS]. Receivables over 90 days total 7.9% of the portfolio [CALCULATED METRICS].

6. **Refinancing purpose.** The facility is requested to refinance a maturing term loan and fund equipment replacement [01_loan_application.pdf#p1]. No new capital injection or business expansion is proposed.

7. **Financial statements reviewed but not audited.** Statements are prepared internally and reviewed annually by an independent accounting firm but are not audited [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1].

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DECLINE**

The borrower fails both critical credit metrics. DSCR of 0.76x is 39% below the 1.25x minimum coverage requirement. Current leverage of 3.53x exceeds policy, and the requested facility would push pro forma leverage to approximately 5.97x—incompatible with Pecos Capital Partners' risk appetite for middle-market lending. The combination of declining EBITDA, cash depletion, and high customer concentration creates unacceptable refinancing risk. The borrower should be advised to reduce debt, improve operating margins, or seek alternative capital structures before reapplying.
