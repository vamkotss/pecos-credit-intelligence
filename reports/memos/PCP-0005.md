## RECOMMENDATION

**DECLINE**

Pro forma leverage of 3.83x EBITDA exceeds policy and no structural mitigant has been proposed. While the borrower demonstrates adequate current coverage and cash generation, the combination of declining revenue momentum, extreme customer concentration (90.2% in top five), and unaudited financials compounds the leverage concern. The facility size ($6.1M) relative to EBITDA ($2.54M) is incompatible with policy parameters for a middle-market senior secured lender. 

Borrower may reapply with: (i) a reduced facility size to achieve pro forma leverage ≤3.5x, or (ii) a structural mitigant such as subordinated equity injection or cash sweep provisions tied to leverage thresholds.
