## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

Total interest-bearing debt is $6,680,366 [CALCULATED METRICS]. Pro forma debt after the $11,000,000 facility would be approximately $17,680,366. Pro forma leverage would be approximately 5.63x EBITDA, materially above the 3.5x policy threshold and without disclosed structural mitigants [CALCULATED METRICS].

Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) is 1.02x, calculated as (EBITDA – capex – taxes) / (interest + principal) = ($3,138,167 – $675,106 – $345,083) / ($614,558 + $1,452,870) [CALCULATED METRICS]. This falls below the 1.25x minimum coverage requirement and indicates minimal headroom for debt service.

Interest coverage is 5.11x (EBITDA / interest expense), which is adequate [CALCULATED METRICS]. However, the tight DSCR reflects the burden of principal repayment, not interest alone.

---

## FINDINGS

**Leverage:** Pro forma total debt / EBITDA of approximately 5.63x exceeds the 3.5x policy threshold. No structural mitigant (asset sale, equity injection, EBITDA covenant, or debt reduction) has been identified.

**Coverage:** DSCR of 1.02x fails the 1.25x minimum requirement. The borrower has minimal cash generation available after capital expenditure and tax obligations to service debt principal.

**Receivables Quality:** Accounts receivable aging shows 3.1% over 90 days, which is acceptable [CALCULATED METRICS]. However, customer concentration is extreme: the top five customers represent 100.0% of receivables, with the largest customer (Pecan Creek Services) at 26.0% [CALCULATED METRICS]. This concentration creates material refinancing risk if any major customer is lost or reduces volume.

**Cash Decline:** Cash fell 49.8% year-over-year despite positive operating cash flow, driven by debt repayment and member distributions [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2] [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]. The borrower is generating cash but deploying it to reduce existing debt rather than building reserves.

**Revenue Trend:** Revenue declined 3.3% while EBITDA grew modestly, indicating cost management but a contracting top line [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1]. The broker email states "FY2025 was a strong year and management expects continued growth," which contradicts the reported revenue decline [07_broker_email_thread.pdf#p1].

**Existing Debt:** The borrower carries $2.4M equipment term debt (Frost Bank, 7.12%, due 2027) and $4.3M owner-occupied CRE mortgage (BancFirst, 9.10%, due 2030) [04_debt_schedule.pdf#p1]. The proposed $11M facility would refinance existing senior debt and fund $2.1M in capital expenditures [01_loan_application.pdf#p1].

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DECLINE**

The transaction fails two critical policy requirements:

1. **Leverage:** Pro forma debt/EBITDA of ~5.63x exceeds the 3.5x threshold with no structural mitigant in place.
2. **Coverage:** DSCR of 1.02x is below the 1.25x minimum, leaving insufficient cash flow to service the proposed facility.

Additionally, extreme customer concentration (100% of receivables from five customers) and a contracting revenue base create refinancing risk. The borrower's cash position has deteriorated sharply despite debt repayment, signaling limited financial flexibility.

The facility size and structure are incompatible with the borrower's current cash generation profile. A smaller facility ($6M–$7M) paired with mandatory equity injection and customer diversification milestones might be reconsidered, but the current request cannot be supported.
