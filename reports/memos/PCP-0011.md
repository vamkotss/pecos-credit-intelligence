# CREDIT MEMORANDUM
## PCP-0011 | Caprock Industries

---

## FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE

Caprock Industries generated revenue of $51,184,615 in FY2025, representing 7.4% growth over the prior year [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1]. Gross margin stands at 50.6% [CALCULATED METRICS], indicating solid pricing power in environmental remediation services.

EBITDA of $3,730,869 grew 3.9% year-over-year [CALCULATED METRICS], but EBITDA margin compressed to 7.3% from prior-year levels [CALCULATED METRICS], reflecting operating expense growth outpacing revenue expansion. Net income reached $1,444,034, a net margin of 2.8% [CALCULATED METRICS].

Interest coverage is adequate at 5.01x [CALCULATED METRICS], supported by declining absolute interest expense ($744,274 in FY2025 vs. $829,480 in FY2024) [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p1] as existing debt amortizes.

## BALANCE SHEET

Total assets of $21,390,126 comprise $11,296,790 in current assets and $10,093,336 in property, plant and equipment [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2]. The current ratio of 2.45x is healthy [CALCULATED METRICS].

Accounts receivable of $5,889,736 represent 11.5% of annual revenue, a reasonable level for the industry. However, concentration is extreme: the top five customers represent 100.0% of receivables [CALCULATED METRICS], with Trinity Bend Distribution alone at 31.1% [CALCULATED METRICS]. Only 4.0% of receivables are over 90 days old [CALCULATED METRICS], indicating acceptable collection performance despite concentration risk.

Cash declined 79.2% over the period to $518,376 [CALCULATED METRICS], driven by capital expenditures of $1,613,259 and debt repayment of $1,065,627 [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]. Operating cash flow of $2,105,854 covered both capex and debt service but left minimal liquidity cushion [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3].

Total equity stands at $9,495,578, representing 44.4% of total capitalization [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p2].

---

## LEVERAGE AND COVERAGE

**Leverage:** Total interest-bearing debt is $8,348,080 [CALCULATED METRICS]. Pro forma debt after the $9,100,000 facility (assuming refinance of existing debt) would be approximately $9,100,000, yielding pro forma leverage of 2.44x EBITDA. This is below the 3.5x policy threshold and requires no structural mitigant. [CALCULATED METRICS baseline: 2.24x]

**Coverage:** Debt Service Coverage Ratio is 0.96x, calculated as (EBITDA $3,730,869 – capex $1,613,259 – taxes $383,857) / (interest $744,274 + principal $1,065,627) [CALCULATED METRICS]. This **fails the 1.25x minimum requirement**. The borrower cannot service debt from operating cash flow under current capex and tax assumptions.

The shortfall reflects elevated capital intensity ($1,613,259 capex in FY2025) and modest EBITDA generation relative to debt service obligations. The borrower funded this gap through cash drawdown and prior-year retained earnings.

## FINDINGS

**Strengths:**
- Stable, 19-year operating history in environmental remediation [01_loan_application.pdf#p1]
- Solid gross margins (50.6%) and revenue growth (7.4%) [CALCULATED METRICS]
- Acceptable leverage at 2.24x pro forma [CALCULATED METRICS]
- Strong current ratio (2.45x) and low receivables aging (4.0% over 90 days) [CALCULATED METRICS]
- Declining absolute interest expense as existing debt amortizes

**Weaknesses:**
- **DSCR of 0.96x fails the 1.25x coverage requirement** [CALCULATED METRICS]
- Extreme customer concentration: top five customers = 100% of receivables, largest customer = 31.1% [CALCULATED METRICS]
- Cash position deteriorated 79.2% year-over-year to $518,376 [CALCULATED METRICS]
- EBITDA margin compression (7.3%) despite revenue growth [CALCULATED METRICS]
- Capex intensity ($1,613,259 annually) consumes 43% of EBITDA [02_financial_statements_comparative.pdf#p3]
- Financial statements are reviewed but not audited [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]
- Operating facility leased from entity controlled by 60% owner (related-party transaction) [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]

**Structural Concerns:**
- Existing debt schedule shows four separate lenders with overlapping collateral (equipment, real property, all business assets, AR/inventory) [04_debt_schedule.pdf#p1], creating refinance complexity
- Proposed $9.1M facility must refinance $8.3M existing debt plus fund $2.1M capex, leaving minimal deleveraging
- No material adverse change reported since year-end, but trading data not independently verified [06_borrower_questionnaire.pdf#p1]

---

## RECOMMENDATION

**DECLINE**

The facility fails the mandatory debt service coverage requirement. DSCR of 0.96x indicates the borrower cannot service the proposed debt from operating cash flow under current operating and capex assumptions. While leverage is acceptable and the business demonstrates stable operations, the coverage shortfall is structural: EBITDA of $3.7M cannot support $1.8M in annual debt service plus $1.6M in capex without cash depletion.

The borrower would need to either (1) reduce capex materially, (2) improve EBITDA margin by 15%+ to reach 1.25x DSCR, or (3) accept a smaller facility. Extreme customer concentration (100% of AR in top five customers) adds execution risk if any major customer is lost.

A revised proposal with lower capex commitments or higher EBITDA guidance, supported by audited financials and customer concentration mitigation, could be reconsidered.
